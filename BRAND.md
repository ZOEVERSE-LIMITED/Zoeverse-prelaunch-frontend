# Zoeverse — Care through your eyes

The review collection experience should feel welcoming, credible and human.
Its first job is to explain what a visitor can do: find a facility and share
an honest account of a visit. Use direct invitations and specific guidance.

## Visual identity

- Deep botanical teal `#153f3b`: principal headings, primary actions, feature sections.
- Ivory `#faf8f1`: the main canvas.
- Sage `#e7eee2`: supportive illustration and quiet surfaces.
- Terracotta `#bd573c`: a restrained editorial accent, never an error substitute.
- Literata: expressive headings; DM Sans: body and interface; DM Mono: short labels.
- Retain the existing lowercase Zoeverse wordmark. The care illustration is
  supporting artwork, not a replacement corporate logo.

## Experience principles

One obvious starting action. Explain the three stages before asking for input.
Keep review forms comfortably narrow, with generous spacing and visible progress.
Use the shared theme for search, identity, questions, consent, legal pages and
confirmation. Preserve keyboard focus and respect reduced-motion preferences.

Never invent testimonials, review counts, approval badges, or care outcomes.
Explain anonymity accurately: the name is collected at the start, while the
submitter chooses how it appears publicly at the end. Avoid promises about
instant publishing or unverified completion times.

Theme implementation: `src/styles/tokens.css` and `src/styles/brand.css`.
The existing API, validation, consent, and submission logic remain in place.
